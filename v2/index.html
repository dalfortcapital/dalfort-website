What We Believe

We don't invest from a distance.
The best investments start with showing up.

Patient Capital

Our investors have long-term horizons with no predetermined hold periods. We exit when our partners agree it's right for the business, not to meet an artificial timeline.

Operator-Led

Our team and operating partners have spent careers running businesses like yours. We bring a deep bench of executives who have navigated the same challenges you face and can share hard-won best practices across operations, sales, and leadership.

Embedded Partnership

We are not ivory tower investors relying on quarterly check-ins. We stay informed so that we can be a resource when you need one, not a bottleneck. We work collaboratively with management to define and execute on a mutually agreed upon value creation plan.

Strategic Growth

We help our partners unlock value by facilitating strategic add-ons, leadership upgrades where gaps exist, systems that professionalize operations, technology that unlocks capacity, and AI tools that improve daily operations. We have sourced, executed, and integrated 14 add-on acquisitions and optimized manufacturing footprints across more than a dozen operating facilities.
