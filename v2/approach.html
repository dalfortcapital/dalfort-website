How We Work

Listening and resources.
An extension of your team.

Before we talk strategy, we listen. We visit your facility, walk the floors, and take the time to learn the people and the culture. What we find shapes everything that follows. We work collaboratively with management to build a value creation plan that reflects your specific business, not a template from another deal. Our operating partners show up at your facility and work through problems shoulder-to-shoulder, bringing experience, pattern recognition, and a deep bench of resources when it matters most. We become an extension of your team, accessible and informed, ready to be a resource when you need one. Strategic add-ons, leadership development, and capability building are part of how we build, always without artificial timelines.

Transaction Types

We pursue equity investments
across multiple scenarios.

Founder & Family Buyouts and Recapitalizations

For most owners, this is the most consequential financial decision they will ever make. We acquire controlling stakes in profitable lower middle market businesses, with every structure built around your specific goals. Whether you are stepping back gradually, finding the right next leader, or simply weighing your options, we build partnerships that honor what you have built and provide the capital and operating capability to grow it.

Corporate Carve-Outs

We acquire divisions or subsidiaries that are non-core to their parent companies, unlocking value through dedicated focus and operational investment.

Strategic Add-Ons

We source and execute bolt-on acquisitions for portfolio companies that expand geography, product lines, or customer reach in a targeted way.

Strategic Growth Capital

For businesses with strong fundamentals and a clear path to growth, we make minority equity investments. If you are not ready to sell or simply want a capital partner to accelerate what you have already built, we can structure a solution that works for you.
