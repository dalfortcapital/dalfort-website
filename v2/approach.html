How We Work

Collaborative, not prescriptive.

01

Listen

We learn your business from the people who know it: you. We visit plants, walk floors, meet customers before we talk strategy.

02

Partner

We co-develop plans with your team, combining your knowledge of the business with our resources and pattern recognition. No prescribed playbook.

03

Operate

Our operating partners show up at your facility and work through problems shoulder-to-shoulder. They stay as long as it takes.

04

Build

Strategic add-ons, new capabilities, talent development. We invest in real growth with no artificial deadlines.

Transaction Types

We pursue control investments
across multiple scenarios.

Control Buyouts & Recapitalizations

We acquire controlling stakes in profitable lower middle market businesses, partnering with founders and management teams to accelerate growth.

Corporate Carve-Outs

We acquire divisions or subsidiaries that are non-core to their parent companies, unlocking value through dedicated focus and operational investment.

Strategic Add-Ons

We source and execute bolt-on acquisitions for portfolio companies that expand geography, product lines, or customer reach in a targeted way.

Founder and Family-Owned

For most owners, this is the most consequential financial decision they will ever make. Every partnership we structure is built around your specific goals, whether that is staying operational, stepping back gradually, or finding the right next leader.

What We Look For

We focus on what a company
can become.

Defensible Market Position

Companies with strong customer relationships, recurring revenue, and a clear reason customers keep coming back year after year.

Operational Complexity

Businesses that require real operational expertise to run well. The kind of complexity that creates a moat when managed by the right team.

Founder-Led Transitions

Entrepreneurs and families looking for a partner who will steward what they've built with the same care they've invested for decades.

Add-On Opportunity

Platforms with the potential to grow through strategic acquisitions that expand geography, product lines, or customer access.
