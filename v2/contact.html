Whether you are thinking about succession, retirement, or simply weighing your options, we are a good first conversation. We work with business owners and intermediaries across precision manufacturing, specialty chemicals, building products, and related industries. If that describes your business, we'd like to hear from you.

What We Look For

Strong core operations with differentiated products and committed leadership. Fragmented end markets where scale, systems, and capability expansion create lasting value.

How We Engage

For most owners, a transaction is the most consequential financial decision they will ever make. We don't take that lightly. We prefer to build relationships over time, getting to know your business, your goals, and what matters to you long before a deal is on the table.

When the timing is right, we can move quickly. If you're early in the process or just weighing your options, we're happy to be a sounding board. We've sat across the table from countless entrepreneurs navigating this for the first time, and we bring that experience to every conversation.
