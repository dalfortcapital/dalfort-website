About

We built this firm to be
the partner we always wanted to have.

DalFort is a Dallas-based investment firm focused on the lower middle market. We partner with founders and management teams to build industrial businesses for the long term.

Our capital is sourced primarily from family offices and high-net-worth individuals, many of them founders and executives within our own target industries. There are no artificial timelines and no forced exits. We preserve what you have built while providing the capital, the team, and the operating capability to grow it.

What Sets Us Apart

Capital with no expiration date.

Patient Capital

Our investors have long-term horizons with no predetermined hold periods. We exit when our partners agree it's right for the business, not to meet an artificial timeline.

Operator-Led

Our team has built and run industrial businesses. When we sit across the table, we've been on your side of it.

Embedded Partnership

Our team and operating partners work on-site alongside you. We are not ivory tower investors relying on quarterly check-ins. We offer real, daily collaboration.

Strategic Growth

We identify and execute add-on acquisitions that strengthen customer relationships and expand into new markets.
