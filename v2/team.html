Team

Operators first, investors second.

We built DalFort Capital around a simple idea: the best investment partners are the ones who've actually run businesses. Every member of our team brings real operating experience to the table, not just capital.

Operating Partners & Advisors

MW

Morris Wheeler

Senior Fund Advisor

Morris Wheeler is the founder and managing member of Drummond Road Capital. He previously served as Chairman and CEO of Cohesant Technologies, where investors realized a 28% annualized return culminating in a sale to Graco Inc. in 2008. BD

Bowen Diehl

Senior Fund Advisor

Bowen Diehl served as President and Chief Executive Officer of Capital Southwest Corporation (NASDAQ: CSWC), a Dallas-based business development company focused on the lower middle market, from 2015 until transitioning to an advisory role in early 2025. He joined Capital Southwest in 2014 as Chief Investment Officer and led the firm's transformation into a credit-focused BDC with nearly $1.7 billion in investments. Prior to Capital Southwest, he served as Managing Director and Co-Head of the Sponsor Finance Group at American Capital, where he closed investments in 15 platform companies representing more than $1.1 billion of invested capital. He began his career in investment banking at Merrill Lynch and Chase Securities. He holds a Bachelor of Engineering from Vanderbilt University and an MBA from the University of Texas at Austin.

Rob Pawlak

Tim Meyer
