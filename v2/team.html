Operating Partners & Advisors

BD

Bowen Diehl

Senior Fund Advisor

Bowen Diehl served as President and Chief Executive Officer and as a member of the Board of Directors of Capital Southwest Corporation (NASDAQ: CSWC), a business development company ("BDC") founded in 1961, from October 2015 through February 2025. Prior to being named President/CEO, Mr. Diehl served as its Chief Investment Officer from March 2014 through September 2015. Mr. Diehl led the transformation of CSWC from an all-equity-focused BDC, trading at a 20% discount to NAV/share, and paying only a negligible annual dividend to shareholders, to a market-leading BDC with a market capitalization of over $1.2 billion, consistently trading in the top decile of public BDCs on a Price/NAV per share basis. As of February 2025, CSWC had investable assets in excess of $2 billion, and an annual dividend to shareholders of $2.56/share (representing an annual yield of 11.1%).

During Mr. Diehl's tenure as President/CEO, CSWC raised over $1.5 billion in debt capital from five different debt capital markets (bank, retail bonds, institutional bonds, SBIC funding, and institutional convertible bonds), as well as over $700 million in public equity capital. During his tenure, CSWC completed 181 transactions, investing $2.6 billion in debt and equity capital, financing buyout transactions with over 100 unique private equity firms. CSWC exited 88 of these investments, representing proceeds of over $1.1 billion and generating a weighted average IRR of 13.7%. Finally, during his tenure, CSWC received Wall Street research coverage, for the first time in its history, from seven analysts and investment-grade credit ratings from Moody's and Fitch.

Prior to joining Capital Southwest, Mr. Diehl was employed by American Capital, Ltd., a publicly traded BDC and global asset manager. From 2007 to 2014, he served as Co-Head of American Capital's Sponsor Finance Group, the group responsible for the majority of American Capital's middle market lending activities.

From 2001 to 2007, Mr. Diehl served as a senior investment professional in the Dallas Office of American Capital. Mr. Diehl sourced, structured, and managed investments that included senior and subordinated debt, as well as preferred and common equity in both control and non-control structures.

Prior to American Capital, Mr. Diehl spent time as an investment banker with Merrill Lynch and Chase Securities, in New York, NY and Houston, TX, where he focused on companies in the exploration and production, oilfield services, natural gas pipeline, natural gas gathering and processing, homebuilding, and semiconductor sectors.

Mr. Diehl earned a Bachelor of Engineering degree, with majors in Civil Engineering and Economics, from Vanderbilt University and a Master of Business Administration from the University of Texas at Austin.

Mr. Diehl serves on the boards of both Highland Homes, a leading master-planned community home builder, headquartered in Plano, Texas and serving the major Texas markets, and Crestline Lending Solutions, a perpetual private BDC, managed by Crestline, a premier alternative asset manager, based in Fort Worth, Texas. Mr. Diehl's community involvement includes serving on the boards of West Dallas Community School (former Chairman), West Dallas Community School Foundation, and Youthworld (afterschool program for underprivileged children in South Dallas).
