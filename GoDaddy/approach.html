How We Work

Collaborative, not prescriptive.

Before we talk strategy, we listen. We visit your facility, walk the floors, and learn the people and the culture that define your business. What we find shapes everything that follows. We work collaboratively with management to build a value creation plan that reflects your business, not a playbook written for a different one. When a challenge calls for it, our operating partners engage directly, bringing experience and pattern recognition from dozens of similar situations. We become an extension of your team, accessible and informed, and ready to act when you need us.

Transaction Types

We pursue equity investments
across multiple scenarios.

Buyouts and Recapitalizations

We acquire controlling stakes in profitable founder and family owned lower middle market businesses. Whether you have reached an inflection point and want a capital partner to accelerate what you have built, are seeking partial liquidity while staying active, or are ready to transition the business entirely, we structure every partnership around your goals.

Corporate Carve-Outs

We acquire divisions or subsidiaries that are non-core to their parent companies. These businesses often carry strong fundamentals but receive limited attention and capital within a larger organization. We unlock their potential through dedicated ownership, focused management, and hands-on operational investment.

Add-On Acquisitions

We source and execute bolt-on acquisitions for portfolio companies that expand geography, product lines, or customer reach in a targeted way.

Strategic Growth Capital

For businesses with strong fundamentals and a clear path to growth, we make minority equity investments. If you are not ready to sell or simply want a capital partner to accelerate what you have already built, we can structure a solution that works for you.
